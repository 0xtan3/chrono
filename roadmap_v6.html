You noticed right: Azure certs are dramatically cheaper in India — AZ-900 is just ₹3,696 (and often FREE via Virtual Training Day vouchers). The strategy: get Azure certs fast for HR resume visibility, then invest in AWS certs for technical depth and salary. Both on your resume is the strongest combination.
💡 Free hack: Microsoft Virtual Training Day events give 50% vouchers for all -900 level exams — AZ-900 becomes ~₹1,848. Complete the Coursera AZ-900 specialisation and get another 50% off voucher. Cloud Skills Challenges on Microsoft Learn can unlock free exam vouchers entirely. Always check events.microsoft.com before paying full price.
